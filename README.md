### Why I Like It

## 2. Midnight Sun

### About the Movie
Midnight Sun is a romantic movie about a girl named Katie who has a rare condition that prevents her from being exposed to sunlight. Her life changes when she meets a boy named Charlie.

### Characters
- Katie Price
- Charlie Reed
- Jack Price
- Morgan

### Why I Like It
I like Midnight Sun because it is a touching movie that shows the meaning of love and making the most of every moment. It reminds me that even simple moments can become special when shared with someone important. I also like how the story teaches us to appreciate life.

![Five Feet Apart](https://placehold.co/400x200?text=Five+Feet+Apart)

## 3. Five Feet Apart

### About the Movie
Five Feet Apart is a romantic drama about two teenagers, Stella and Will, who have cystic fibrosis and must stay six feet apart to avoid getting infections from each other.

### Characters
- Stella Grant
- Will Newman
- Poe Ramirez
- Barb

### Why I Like It
I like Five Feet Apart because it shows how difficult situations cannot stop people from caring about one another. The story is emotional and teaches the importance of love, patience, and understanding. It also reminds me to value the people who make life meaningful.
